Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What the games store

Neon Drift saves your progress (best score, crystals, ships you own, daily challenge progress and your settings, such as sound, volume and frame rate) in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Accounts and personal information

The games and this website have no accounts and never ask for your name, email address or any other personal information.

Advertising

This website may show ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.

Read more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the itch.io version of Neon Drift is hosted by itch.io. Like any web host, they may log technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure their service. See the GitHub privacy statement and the itch.io privacy policy.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages and collect no personal information. Ads on this website are provided by Google under its own policies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: leave a comment on the Neon Drift itch.io page or open an issue on GitHub.
