Neon Drift

Click the game once so it receives your key presses. Open it full screen for the best view, especially on phones (landscape works best).

About the game

Neon Drift is an endless runner. Your ship moves forward by itself and keeps getting faster, from 430 to 880 world units per second, reaching top speed after about 2,800 meters. You decide when to jump and when to dash. The level is built as you go, so no two runs are the same, but every gap is generated to be clearable at your current speed.

Controls

ActionKeyboardTouchGamepad
Jump / double jumpSpace, ↑ or WTap the left halfA
DashShift, → or DTap the right halfB, X or RB
PauseP or Esc❚❚ buttonStart
CRT filter / muteC / MMenu buttons

Hold jump for a full jump and tap it for a short hop. The game also forgives near misses: you can still jump for 0.09 seconds after running off a ledge, and a jump pressed up to 0.13 seconds before landing is remembered.

Hazards at a glance

  • Red spikes and saws always hurt. Jump over them.
  • Amber gates and drones break if you dash into them, and smashing them is worth points.
  • Falling off the bottom of the screen ends the run, even with a shield.

Features

  • Levels generated as you play, tuned to the jump physics
  • Combo multiplier up to x8, a daily challenge with the same target for everyone, and a login streak
  • Music and sound effects synthesized in the browser, with no audio files
  • Optional CRT scanline filter, separate music and effects volume
  • 30 FPS, 60 FPS or Unlimited frame rate. Slow devices switch to 30 FPS automatically.
  • Progress saved in your browser. No account needed.
