Ship powers explained

Updated 28 October 2026 · about 5 minutes to read

Voltdart has five ships. They all handle the same way, but each one has a single power that changes how you play. Here's exactly what each power does, with the numbers from the game.

Prices are in crystals, which you earn by playing (see Scoring and crystals). For scale, the playing area is 720 units tall and a full jump rises about 166 units.

Drifter: Combo Keeper

The starting ship. Its combo timer lasts 3.2 seconds instead of 2.2. That sounds small, but it's 45% more time, and it covers most of the quiet stretches between orb lines. Since the multiplier only rises every 5 pickups in a row, keeping a combo alive is what gets you to x5 and beyond. Drifter is a strong ship for high scores, not just a beginner ship.

Viper: Long Dash

Dashes last 40% longer (0.31 instead of 0.22 seconds) and recharge in 0.6 seconds instead of 0.9. In practice you can dash about one and a half times as often, and each dash carries you further. Because a dash keeps your height, a longer dash also crosses wider gaps. Viper is the best ship for smashing amber gates and drones, which are worth 30 and 50 points each, times your multiplier.

Solar: Super Magnet

Every ship pulls in orbs that pass close by. Solar's magnet reaches 240 units instead of 130, almost twice as far, and pulls a little faster. Orbs you would normally miss by a jump's width get collected anyway, which makes combos much easier to keep going. A caught orb always reaches the ship within a fraction of a second.

Phantom: Shield

Phantom starts every run with a shield, shown as a slowly turning hexagon around the ship. The first time you hit a hazard, the shield breaks instead of you, and you get 1.2 seconds of safety to recover. It works against red hazards, amber hazards and walls, but not against falling off the bottom of the screen. It refreshes every run, so it's like a free second chance each time.

Prism: Triple Jump

Prism gets a third jump in mid-air, and its color cycles through the rainbow. A third jump lets you fix a mistimed jump, climb to high platforms, or wait in the air for a hazard to pass. Combined with the air dash (which gives one jump back once you've used two), Prism can stay airborne for a long time.

Which ship should you buy first?

  1. Phantom (900) if you're still learning. One free crash per run means longer runs, and longer runs earn more crystals.
  2. Viper (300) if you want something early. It's the cheapest, and smashing hazards adds a lot of points.
  3. Solar (600) if you chase combos and crystals, since more orbs means more of both.
  4. Prism (1,500) last. It's the most fun to move with, but the triple jump matters less once your timing is good.

Drifter stays a good choice the whole time. If you're good at keeping combos alive, its longer timer can beat the other powers for score.
