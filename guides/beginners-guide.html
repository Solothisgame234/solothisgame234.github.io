Voltdart beginner's guide

Updated 28 October 2026 · about 5 minutes to read

Voltdart only has two buttons, jump and dash, but how and when you press them matters a lot. This guide covers everything you need for your first good runs.

The two buttons

KeyboardTouchGamepad
JumpSpace, ↑ or WTap the left half of the screenA
DashShift, → or DTap the right half of the screenB, X or RB

Your ship always flies forward on its own. Pause with P, Esc, the ❚❚ button or Start.

Read the colors

Color is how the game tells you what to do, and it never changes its meaning:

  • Red means jump. Spikes and saws always end your run if you touch them, even while dashing.
  • Amber means dash. Gates and drones break if you dash into them, and smashing them gives points (30 for a gate, 50 for a drone, times your multiplier). Touching one without dashing counts as a crash.
  • Falling below the screen ends the run, whatever ship you use.

The background slowly changes between cyan and violet as your score rises, but it never turns red or amber, so danger always stands out.

Short hops and full jumps

How long you hold jump decides how high you go. Hold it for a full jump. Let go early and the ship falls back quickly, which gives you a short hop. Short hops are the right move for low spikes because you land sooner and have more time for whatever comes next.

Two small helps make timing easier:

  • Late jumps still count. For 0.09 seconds after you run off a ledge, you can still make a normal jump.
  • Early presses are remembered. If you press jump up to 0.13 seconds before landing, the ship jumps the moment it touches down.

Double jump

Press jump again in mid-air for a second jump. The ship flips forward as it happens. A double jump at the top of your first jump gives you the most height and distance, so use it for the widest gaps and highest platforms rather than right away.

Dashing

A dash is a short burst at almost twice your speed (1.9×) that lasts 0.22 seconds and needs 0.9 seconds to recharge. The bar under your score shows when it's ready. Three things to know:

  • While dashing, the ship keeps its height and doesn't fall, so a dash in mid-air carries you across gaps.
  • If you've already used your double jump, an air dash gives you one jump back.
  • Dashing into amber hazards breaks them. There's a tiny grace window at the end of a dash, so a dash that ends just before a gate still counts.

Orbs and the combo

Green orbs are worth points and crystals. Collecting orbs (or smashing amber hazards) one after another builds a combo. Every 5 in a row raises your multiplier by one, up to x8. If you go 2.2 seconds without collecting anything, the combo resets. Orb lines usually follow the path of a good jump, so following the orbs is also a hint for how to jump.

Second chances

When you crash, you may be offered a Second Chance: spend a token or 60 crystals within 6 seconds to keep going. It's offered once per run and resets your combo. You can also say no and keep your crystals.

Good first goals

  1. Reach 1,000 points. That's roughly 1,000 meters plus some orbs.
  2. Get an x2 multiplier by collecting 5 orbs in a row.
  3. Smash your first amber gate with a dash.
  4. Beat the daily challenge target shown on the start screen (between 1,500 and 4,000) for a 50-crystal bonus.
  5. Save 300 crystals for Viper, or read Ship powers explained first.

If the game feels choppy

Open the start screen or pause menu and choose 30 FPS. A steady 30 frames per second often feels smoother than an unsteady 60 on older phones and laptops. If your device clearly can't keep up, the game switches to 30 FPS on its own during your first run.
