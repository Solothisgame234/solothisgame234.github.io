Scoring and crystals

Updated 28 October 2026 · about 6 minutes to read

Your score comes from two places: distance and bonus points. Crystals, the game's currency, are worked out from your score and orbs when the run ends. Here's how both work.

Where points come from

SourcePoints
Distance1 point per meter (2 per meter during Head Start)
Orb10 × multiplier
Amber gate smashed30 × multiplier
Amber drone smashed50 × multiplier

Distance points add up steadily, but bonus points are where big scores come from. At x8, one orb is worth 80 points, about as much as 80 meters of flying.

How the multiplier works

Each orb you collect and each hazard you smash adds 1 to your combo. Your multiplier is 1 plus one for every 5 in the combo, up to x8:

Combo0–45–910–1415–1920–2425–2930–3435+
Multiplierx1x2x3x4x5x6x7x8

Every pickup restarts the combo timer: 2.2 seconds normally, 3.2 seconds with Drifter. When the timer runs out, the combo and the multiplier drop back to zero and x1. The thin bar under the multiplier at the top of the screen shows how much time is left.

Tips for keeping a combo alive:

  • Follow the orb lines. They trace the path of a well-timed jump, so collecting them and jumping well go together.
  • Smash amber hazards. They count toward the combo too.
  • Solar's bigger magnet and Drifter's longer timer both make long combos much easier. See Ship powers explained.

Head Start

Head Start comes in packs of 3 for 120 crystals. When it's switched on, your next run starts with 30 seconds of double score: distance counts twice, and orbs and smashes count at least x2. If your combo multiplier is already higher than x2, the higher one is used, so the two don't stack.

Crystals at the end of a run

RewardCrystals
Orbs collected1 per 4 orbs
Score2 per 1,000 points
New personal best+25
Daily challenge beaten (once a day)+50
Supporter Pack owners+25% of the total

There are two more ways that don't need a run:

  • Daily login bonus: 20 crystals on the first day of a streak, rising by 10 a day to 70 from day 6 onward. Missing a day restarts the streak.
  • Developer message: every few runs the game may offer a 5-second message from the developer. Watching it gives 15 crystals, up to 5 times a day. You can always skip it.

The daily challenge

Each day has a target score between 1,500 and 4,000. Everyone playing on the same date gets the same target. Beat it once to earn 50 crystals. It resets at midnight on your device, and the start screen shows how long is left.

What to spend crystals on

Ships are the best long-term buy because they never run out. Tokens are cheaper than reviving on the spot (50 instead of 60), so if you like to revive, buy them ahead of time.
