NEON DRIFT

A fast neon endless runner. Jump, double-jump and dash through a glowing synthwave city, chain orbs into combos and see how far you can go.

PLAY NOW Also on itch.io

How to play

  • Jump: Space, W or the up arrow, or tap the left side of the screen. Press again in the air for a double jump. Hold it to jump higher.
  • Dash: Shift, D or the right arrow, or tap the right side of the screen.
  • Pause: P or Esc. Gamepads work too (A to jump, B or X to dash).
  • Red hazards (spikes and saws) always end your run. Amber gates and drones break if you dash through them.
  • Collect orbs quickly one after another to build a combo multiplier up to x8.
  • Every run earns Neon Crystals. Spend them in the shop on new ships, a head start or a second chance.

Tips

  • Short taps give small hops. Save your double jump for the big gaps.
  • A dash in mid-air keeps your height, so you can use it to cross wide gaps.
  • The daily challenge changes every day. Beat its target score for bonus crystals.
