Neon Drift

A fast endless runner set in a glowing synthwave city. Jump, double-jump and dash past red hazards, smash amber ones, and chain orbs into an x8 combo.

Free, no download, no sign-up. It runs in any modern browser on a computer, phone or tablet, and works with a keyboard, touch or a gamepad.

PLAY NOW   How to play

What makes Neon Drift different

  • Every gap can be cleared. The level is generated as you play, and every gap is measured against the jump physics at your current speed. If you crash, it was the timing, not bad luck. How that works.
  • Five ships with real differences. Each ship has its own power, from a longer combo timer to a triple jump. Compare them.
  • Colors you can read at speed. Red always means "this kills you" and amber always means "dash through it". The rest of the world shifts between cyan and violet as your score climbs, so the two danger colors never blend in.
  • Runs well on weak devices. Pick 30 FPS, 60 FPS or Unlimited in the menu. Slow devices switch to a steady 30 FPS by themselves.
  • No real-money shop inside the game. Crystals are earned by playing. Supporting the game is optional and happens on itch.io.
